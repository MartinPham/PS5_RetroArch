# PS5 RetroArch v0.5.0-alpha.5

Native RetroArch for jailbroken PS5 consoles. This release changes the graphics
driver: the title now renders through **RADV**, Mesa's Vulkan driver for AMD
GPUs, ported to the PS5 in [PS5_Vulkan](https://github.com/mihawk-99/PS5_Vulkan).
It replaces ps5vk, the driver every earlier release used.

> **Alpha.** The games and paths below are the ones I have tested on my console.
> Other games may still fail. Please report them (see *Reporting problems*).

## Highlights

- **A new graphics driver.** RADV is the driver Linux uses on AMD GPUs, with its
  ACO shader compiler. It is built from my Mesa fork
  ([PS5_Mesa](https://github.com/mihawk-99/PS5_Mesa)) with a PS5 layer for memory,
  command submission and display, and it reports Vulkan 1.4. The menu, the five
  software cores and the three GPU cores (PPSSPP, Dolphin, LRPS2) all render
  through it.
- **PSP anti-aliasing.** PPSSPP's MSAA works. It needs features ps5vk never had
  (render pass 2 and depth/stencil resolve), so PPSSPP's MSAA setting was
  previously ignored.
- **A shader cache on RADV.** Compiled pipelines are kept in
  `radv-shader-cache/` in the title folder. A game's second start reads them back
  instead of compiling: with the cache filled, PPSSPP's God of War: Ghost of
  Sparta created its pipelines in 29 ms instead of 1.4 s.
- **Faster shader compiles.** Every allocation in the title used to wait on one
  lock, and the emulators compile shaders on several threads at once. The title's
  heap now gives each thread an arena of its own. PPSSPP compiling from an empty
  cache: 2.0 s instead of 5.5 s, with no speed lost after boot.
- **A smaller package.** `sce_module/libvulkan.so.1` is gone: RADV is linked into
  `eboot.bin`, and nothing loaded that library (a PS5 title cannot load one).

## Tested on RADV for this release

On my console, with V-Sync on and a 120 Hz display:

- **Every core with a game**: FCEUmm (Super Mario Bros.), Snes9x (A Link to the
  Past), mGBA (Mario Pinball Land), Genesis Plus GX (Sonic the Hedgehog), FinalBurn
  Neo (Metal Slug), PPSSPP (God of War: Ghost of Sparta), Dolphin (The Wind Waker)
  and LRPS2 (the God of War II demo). Each ran through boot, the menu opened and
  closed, Close Content from the Quick Menu, and the same game loaded again, with
  Threaded Video on for four of them. No crash and no fatal signal, three times
  over as the driver changed during the release work.
- **PPSSPP for ten minutes** with Threaded Video and the menu opened 25 times: no
  crash, every 10-second audio window at 98–100% speed.
- **The pictures**: God of War: Ghost of Sparta and GTA San Andreas show the same
  frames as on ps5vk (the rain and the video move with timing); Wind Waker's title
  screen renders correctly.
- **Speed after boot**: PPSSPP and Dolphin (Wind Waker) hold full speed once the
  game has booted. LRPS2 (GTA San Andreas) does too, but for one late 10-second
  window at 98.9%, where ps5vk had one at 99.4%.

The longer runs listed for v0.4.0-alpha.4 (Wind Waker for an hour, Resident Evil 4
for 30 minutes, Super Smash Bros. Melee, Mario Kart Wii, Rogue Leader, Yu-Gi-Oh!
GX Tag Force, the Final Fantasy X demo) were on ps5vk and have not been repeated
on RADV yet.

## Fixed during the release work

| Problem | Status |
| --- | --- |
| PPSSPP crashed within four seconds of starting a game with MSAA on | **Fixed** in PPSSPP. It took the GPU's Vulkan version as usable although RetroArch opens Vulkan 1.1, so it called a Vulkan 1.2 function RADV correctly withheld. ps5vk never reached that path. |
| Slow first compiles on several threads | **Fixed**: the title heap's arenas (above). |
| A filled shader cache still slow to read from several threads | **Fixed** in the driver: each cache part is opened once, where every read used to reopen and lock its files. |

## Known issues

- **Dolphin's first start of a game.** With an empty shader cache, Dolphin
  compiles its ubershaders while the game boots. Wind Waker's first 20 seconds ran
  at 83% and 92% speed; ps5vk managed 86% and 99% from an empty cache. Later
  starts read the cache and match ps5vk. Making the first start as fast is the
  next piece of work.
- **Rogue Leader** ran at 72–85% for stretches of its attract sequence on ps5vk.
  It has not been measured on RADV yet.
- **60 Hz displays.** The driver measures the refresh rate after switching to
  120 Hz and returns to 60 Hz if the display did not follow, as Alpha 4 did. My
  display is 120 Hz, so this path still needs confirming on a 60 Hz TV.
- **PAL PS2 games** (50 fps) on a 120 Hz output alternate two and three refreshes
  per frame, which can look uneven. The console offers no 50/100 Hz mode.
- **PS2 games in `.7z` archives** are not unpacked. Use ISO, CHD, CSO or another
  supported disc format.
- Mario Party (Wii): black flicker on the world map was reported and has not been
  investigated yet.

## Install or update

1. Copy the complete `PPSA99169` folder to your homebrew title location, for
   example `/data/homebrew/PPSA99169/`, and launch it from your homebrew launcher.
2. Updating keeps your `config/`, `savefiles/`, `savestates/`, `system/` and
   `content/`. Settings already saved there, such as a core's internal
   resolution, are kept rather than replaced by new defaults.
3. Put games in `content/` and BIOS files in `system/` (PS2: `system/pcsx2/bios/`).
   No games or BIOS files are included.
4. After updating from an earlier release, `sce_module/libvulkan.so.1` and
   `ps5vk-shader-cache/` are no longer used and can be deleted. The first start of
   each game compiles its shaders again, into `radv-shader-cache/`.

## Reporting problems

Include the core, game, file format and the settings you changed. After the
problem, and **before relaunching**, copy `trace.txt` and `retroarch.log` from the
title folder: a new launch replaces them.
