## PS5 RetroArch is a passion project, not a piracy project.

It exists so you can play the games you own on the console you own.

- **Piracy is not condoned.**
- **No games, BIOS files, console firmware or decryption keys are included, and they never will be.**
- **Use only legally obtained backups of games you own**, made yourself from your own discs, cartridges or digital purchases.
- **Use only BIOS and firmware files dumped from hardware you own.**
- **Requests for, or links to, games, BIOS files, firmware or keys are not welcome** in this project's issues or discussions.

## Your games, beautifully organized

The new **Games** section turns the WebUI into a visual library for your collection.

- **Find your next game.** Search by name, choose a system, show only games missing media, or jump through **# / A–Z**.
- **Sort it your way.** Alphabetical order, release date, system, genre, developer, publisher, or rating—with ascending and descending controls. Your sorting preferences are remembered, and missing details stay at the end.
- **Choose the look.** Display front covers, 3D boxes, logos, or cartridge/disc artwork on your game cards.
- **Take a closer look.** Open a game’s media collection, enlarge images, play video previews, and read its downloaded details.
- **Make it yours.** Upload replacement media for individual games. Your manually uploaded artwork stays protected from automatic scraping, and transparent images retain their transparency.

## Download Media. Fill in the missing pieces.

A dedicated **Download Media** section brings artwork and game information directly into the shared library.

**Three sources, in your preferred order:** libretro thumbnails, ScreenScraper, and LaunchBox Games Database. Each later source fills what the earlier sources could not find.

- Download for **selected games, one system, or your entire library**.
- Choose front and back covers, 3D boxes, screenshots, title screens, logos, cartridge/disc images, fanart, PDF manuals, and video previews. Availability depends on the source and game.
- Download **game details independently of artwork**: descriptions, release dates, developers, publishers, genres, player counts, and ratings where available.
- Set your preferred region and supported metadata language.
- Keep existing scraped media and download only what is missing, or replace it. Manually uploaded artwork remains protected.
- Sign in to ScreenScraper from the WebUI; account credentials stay on the console.
- Review uncertain matches, search again, select the correct result, or skip a game.
- Follow each source’s progress and review a completion recap of **downloaded, retained, and missing** items.
- Resume interrupted jobs without repeating completed work. Active PS5 download jobs can continue after the title closes.
- Download on the **PS5**, or use the **PC helper** for supported jobs. ScreenScraper and combined-source jobs currently run on the PS5.

The engine now uses up to **32 workers**, while respecting each source’s account and concurrency limits. Fixes also prevent duplicate processing, stranded games, and premature job completion.

## A stylus in your DualSense

DeSmuME and Azahar now use the DualSense touchpad for touchscreen control, with the same smooth white stylus, dark outline, and precise nib in both cores.

| Gesture | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **Slide a finger** | Move the stylus without touching the emulated screen |
| **Physically press the touchpad** | Touch the screen at the nib |
| **Hold the press and slide** | Drag across the touchscreen |
| **Release the press** | Lift the stylus |

Lifting and repositioning your finger avoids a cursor jump. Normal menu and other-core touchpad behavior is preserved. **Touch-surface tap-to-click is not implemented.**

## Bring your storage. Keep your settings.

- **USB and extended drives:** supported drives mounted through ShadowMountPlus appear as browser roots and participate in library discovery. This support was verified with ShadowMountPlus 1.7beta4.
- **More flexible memory:** the title now requests **1 GiB**, up from 448 MiB. This reallocates part of the direct-memory pool; it does not increase the console’s total memory.
- **Working controller remaps:** per-game and per-core remaps now save and load from the correct folder.
- **FTP access repaired:** permission repair completes before frontend handovers, preventing files from being left inaccessible.

## EmulationStation, with the rough edges smoothed out

- Games copied into recognized content folders can appear without creating a RetroArch playlist first, including games on supported mounted drives.
- Playlist entries and discovered files are combined without duplicate paths; existing playlist entries take precedence.
- Expanded platform recognition, folder-based DOS and ScummVM discovery, and filtering of BIOS files and subordinate disc tracks.
- Fixed video previews that displayed a black frame because an FFmpeg audio filter was missing. Preview video and audio now play.
- Shared artwork and metadata feed ES-DE’s game lists.
- The **Scraper** menu entry is replaced by **WebUI** access.

## A WebUI that stays with you

The WebUI now runs as a separate background service and remains available across the picker, RetroArch, and ES-DE. The background service needs the console’s supported ELF loader or homebrew launcher; without one, the fallback is available while RetroArch runs.

- **Faster uploads:** parallel transfers, batches of small files, multipart uploads, HTTP range downloads, and six browser transfer lanes.
- **Measured on the test connection:** approximately **26–30 MB/s**, up from roughly 13 MB/s; small-file throughput approximately doubled. Actual speeds depend on your network.
- **Complete files only:** uploads become visible at their destination when complete; cancelled partial uploads are cleaned up.
- **Large downloads fixed:** resolved transfers stopping after approximately 33 KB.
- **More reliable startup:** fixed slow initialization causing the service to lose its frontend connection and shut down.
- **Concurrent HTTPS fixed:** corrected a threading issue that could crash the service during parallel downloads.
- **Clear update state:** a restart notice appears when the page files are newer than the running server.
- **Broader Alerts:** required BIOS/system-file checks now cover all installed cores, with corrected per-core requirements. These checks verify presence, not authenticity.

## Scan the code. Open your WebUI.

QR codes are generated from the console’s current IP address.

| Frontend | Where to find it |
| --- | --- |
| **Picker** | Press **Triangle**; press Triangle again or Circle to close |
| **RetroArch XMB** | **WebUI** tab immediately left of Settings |
| **RetroArch RGUI** | **WebUI** at the top of the main menu |
| **ES-DE** | **WebUI** in the main menu |

You can also open `http://<PS5-IP>:6769` from a phone or computer on the same local network.

## Upgrading from 0.6.7

1. **Back up your existing title folder**, especially configuration, saves, states, system files, content, and any custom media.
2. Merge the complete `PPSA99169` folder from the beta package into your installation. Preserve your existing user files and configuration.
3. Fully close and reopen the title so the updated executables and WebUI service can start.
4. Open **Games** to explore your collection, or **Download Media** to fill in its artwork and details.

**No games, BIOS files, console firmware, or decryption keys are supplied. Use only legally obtained backups of games you own and system files dumped from hardware you own.**
