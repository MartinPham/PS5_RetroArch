PS5 RetroArch - legal use
=========================

PIRACY IS NOT CONDONED. This title contains no games, no BIOS files, no
console firmware and no decryption keys, and none will ever be provided or
linked to. Use only games you own, as backups you made yourself from your own
discs, cartridges or digital purchases, and BIOS or firmware files dumped from
hardware you own. Downloading or sharing games, BIOS files, firmware or keys
you do not own is piracy: do not do it, and do not ask for help with it.

PLAYSTATION 3 (RPCS3) IS NOT IN ANY RELEASE. RPCS3 is licensed
GPL-2.0-only; this port's code is GPL-3.0-or-later, and the two cannot be
distributed together as one program. RPCS3's source is public
(https://github.com/mihawk-99/PS5_RPCS3) and this project's build scripts
build it: if you want it, you must compile the title from source yourself,
for your own console (README.md, "Build from source"). Do not share the
binaries you build. RPCS3 also needs your own PS3 system software
(PS3UPDAT.PUP), your own games and their licence files, under the same rules.
